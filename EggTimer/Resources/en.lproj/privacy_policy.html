Privacy Policy

EggTime (the "App") values your privacy and handles only the minimum information necessary. The App does not require sign-up or login, and the developer does not collect or store your personal information on any server. To keep the App free, however, the App shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google, the ad provider, may collect some information in the process (see Section 5).

1.Personal Information We Collect and Use

The developer does not directly collect any personal information that can identify you while you use the service. All of the information below that is created in the App is stored only on your device; it is never transmitted externally and cannot be viewed by the developer.

  • Your bookmarked recipes
  • The state of your running timer (selected egg type, remaining time, etc.)
  • Your sound mode and timer end sound settings
  • Your app language setting
  • Your EggTime records shown in widgets (today's soft- and hard-boiled egg counts, and the days you completed this week)

This information is deleted from your device when you delete the App. Information processed to show ads is described in Section 5.

2.Information Processed When You Contact Us

If you send us an email through "Contact Us" in the App, the following information may be automatically included in the email body to help us respond to your inquiry.

  • Device model · iOS version · App version
  • Your device's language/region settings
  • Your email address, if you choose to send the email

This information is sent only if you send the email yourself and is used solely to review and respond to your inquiry. Whether to send it is entirely up to you.

3.Network Requests for Version Checks

To check for the latest version on the App Info screen, the App queries Apple's App Store server with the App's identifier (bundle ID). No personal information is transmitted in this process.

4.Notifications

The App uses local notifications on your device to let you know when your timer is done. You can change whether you receive notifications at any time in your device's Settings, and no personal information is collected in connection with notifications.

5.Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC ("Google"), to show banner ads. When ads are requested and shown, Google may automatically collect and process the information below. The developer does not use this information to identify you.

  • Device information: device model, OS version, language settings, etc.
  • Network information: IP address and the approximate location (country or city level) estimated from it
  • Ad interaction information: records of ad impressions, clicks, and other interactions with ads
  • Diagnostic information: errors and performance data from showing ads

This information is used to show ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud such as invalid clicks. The App does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not ask you for permission to track. For details on how Google handles this information, see the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

International Transfer of Personal Information

  • Recipient: Google LLC (1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA)
  • Destination: the United States and other countries where Google processes data
  • Information transferred: the advertising information listed above
  • When and how: sent over the network each time an ad is requested and shown while you use the App
  • Purpose: showing ads, measuring ad performance, and preventing fraud
  • Retention: as set out in the Google Privacy Policy

The developer does not sell your information or share it with third parties for any purpose other than the advertising described above, and does not use any user behavior analytics tools other than advertising.

6.Retention and Deletion

Information stored on your device is deleted when you delete the App. Information Google collects while showing ads is retained and deleted in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy. Emails received for inquiries are retained and deleted in accordance with applicable laws after the inquiry has been resolved.

7.Your Rights

You can delete the App's data stored on your device at any time by deleting the App. If you would like to access or delete information you sent us through an inquiry, please contact us at the address below.

8.Children's Privacy

The App is not directed primarily at children, and the developer does not directly collect personal information from any users, including children.

9.Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated due to changes in laws or the service. We will notify you through the App when it is updated.
